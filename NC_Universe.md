# The *universe*

The understanding of the scale of the universe expanded through a *sequence of overlapping methods* used to measure *increasingly distant* objects.

### 1. Measuring *Earth* ... 


- **Distance to the Moon** (c. 270 BC). *Aristarchus of Samos* used the geometry of lunar eclipses to <ins>estimate</ins> the <ins>*Moon’s distance*</ins>.  
- **Earth’s Circumference** (c. 240 BC). *Eratosthenes* compared the angle of shadows  to calculate <ins>***Earth's size***<ins>.

### 2. and the *Solar System*

- **Nuremberg Chronicle** (1493). The universe has, according to *Plinius Secundus* (79 AD) and Pythagoras, the <ins>*size of*</ins> the <ins>*Saturn System*</ins> .  
- **Cosmographia** (1568). Based on the *Planetary Hypotheses* from *Ptolemey*, *Velho's*, universe has the <ins>*size of*</ins> the <ins>*inner Solar System*</ins> to Mars.
- **Relative Planetary Scale** (1609 - 1619). *Johannes Kepler* used *Tycho Brahe’s* observations to prove <ins>planetary</ins> orbits are  linked to its <ins>*relative distance*</ins> from the Sun.
- **The Astronomical Unit** (1672). *Giovanni Cassini* using Mars parallax (and later the transit of Venus  1761 - 1769) established the first accurate <ins>*absolute distance to* ***the Sun***</ins>.

### 3. Reaching the *Stars*

- **Stellar Parallax** (1838). *Friedrich Bessel* measured the apparent shift of star 61 Cygni, proving <ins>*stars are vastly distant*</ins> suns.
- **Standard Candles** (1912). *Henrietta Swan Leavitt* discovered the period-luminosity relationship in <ins>*Cepheid*</ins> variable stars, to determine a star's true brightness from its pulsation rate and to calculate the <ins>*luminosity* distance</ins>.

### 4. *Galaxies* and the *universe*

- **Mapping the Galaxy** (1920). *Harlow Shapley* used globular clusters and variable stars to measure the <ins>*size of the Milky Way*</ins>.
- **Beyond the Milky Way** (1924). *Edwin Hubble* identified *Cepheid* variables inside <ins>Andromeda</ins>, proving it was an entirely <ins>*separate galaxy*</ins> far outside.
- **Cosmic Expansion** (1929). Combining distance measurements with galactic <ins>*redshifts*</ins>, *Hubble* demonstrated that galaxies are moving away (*Hubble's Law*), proving <ins>***the universe***</ins> is expanding.

### 5. The *<ins>expanding</ins> universe*
- **The Cosmic Microwave Background** (1965). *Arno Penzias* and *Robert Wilson* detected the thermal echo of the *Big Bang*, to <ins>*date the universe's*</ins> beginning.
- **Dark Energy and Acceleration** (1998). Observations of distant Type Ia supernovae revealed that the expansion of the <ins>*universe is accelerating*</ins>.
- **Precision Cosmology** (2013). Data from the *Planck* satellite, gravitational wave detectors, and the *James Webb* Space Telescope <ins>*refined the age*</ins> of the *observable* universe to 13.8 Ga and its radius to 14.25 Gpc.
